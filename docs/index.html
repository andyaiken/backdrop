Backdrop: Stage Projections

A cue-driven projection console for live theatre, for the Mac.

SupportPrivacy policy

An operator fires cues from a console on the Mac's own display while the projector window runs full screen on a second display. Cues can be titles, text, images, morphs, maps, anagrams, decryption reveals, keypads, video clips and live camera feeds.

Another machine in the booth, such as QLab or a lighting desk, can fire cues over MIDI Show Control or OSC. Backdrop needs macOS 26 or later.
