Getting started

  1. A new show opens in edit mode. Add cues with Add Cue in the header or from the Edit menu.
  2. Choose Show › Enter Show Mode, then Show › Open Projector.
  3. Press Space to fire the standby cue.

Show control

MIDI Show Control and OSC are off until turned on in Settings. OSC listens on UDP port 53100 by default, with addresses such as /backdrop/go/12. macOS may ask whether Backdrop may accept incoming connections; allow it, or nothing from another machine will arrive.
