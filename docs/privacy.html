Privacy policy

Backdrop: Stage Projections. Last updated 11 November 2026.

HomeSupport

Backdrop collects no personal data, has no accounts, and does not track you. It contains no analytics or advertising, and it sends nothing about you or your shows to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your Mac

Your shows, with their pictures and clips, are saved only where you choose to save them. Backdrop's settings are stored on your Mac in its own preferences.

Permissions Backdrop may ask for

  • Camera: only for live feed cues. The picture is shown on stage and is not recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Incoming network connections: only for OSC show control, which is off until you turn it on in Settings. It accepts commands from machines on your network and sends nothing out.
  • Files you open or drop in: Backdrop can read only the shows and media you give it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
